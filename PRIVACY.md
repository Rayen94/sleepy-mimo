# Privacy Policy – Sleepy Mimo

Sleepy Mimo is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Sleepy Mimo YouTube channel to upload videos to that channel.

- It accesses only the owner's own YouTube channel, through the YouTube Data API, to upload videos and read basic channel statistics.
- It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data from other people.
- Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
